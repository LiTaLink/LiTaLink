Go in with a plan, not just guesses about questions. This builds a Strategic Brief on the role and organisation, then drafts your "Tell us about yourself" answer using the SEAT method (Summary, Experience, Achievements, Tie it back) — grounded in your resume and the job description below.

AI-generated — treat it as a starting point, not a script. Sense-check the assumptions against what you actually know about the role, and rehearse the SEAT answer in your own words rather than reading it out.

Before we use AI on your resume

Import, ATS check, interview prep, cover letter, job recommendations and bullet rewriting send the text of your resume (name, contact details, work history, skills) to third-party AI providers so they can process it. Your photo is not sent. AI output can be inaccurate.
