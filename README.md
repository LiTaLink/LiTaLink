## 👋 About

LLiTaLink is a print-and-delivery service built for convenience. Upload your documents, pick a delivery window, and track your order from start to finish, all from one web app.

## ✨ What you can do

- 🖨️ **Print documents**: upload PDF, Word, Excel or image files (up to 10MB each) and choose black & white, colour or laminating
- 🪪 **Photocopy your ID**: snap the front and back of your card and we combine them onto a single printable page
- ✏️ **Order stationery**: shop stationery items separately from print orders
- 🖼️ **Wall art inspo**: pick wall art designs and order them the same way
- 🚚 **Choose a delivery time**: select your area and an available delivery window
- 📍 **Track every order**: follow the status from Pending to Delivered

## 🧩 Built with

- HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript
- [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) for the database, file storage and login
- [JsBarcode](https://github.com/lindell/JsBarcode) for order labels
- [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) for simple, cookie-free visit stats
- Hosted on GitHub Pages

## 🔒 Privacy

We only collect what we need to print, deliver and support your orders, and we never sell your information. LLiTaLink follows South Africa's **POPIA**. You can delete your account at any time from the account menu. Full details are in the Privacy Notice & Terms inside the app.
